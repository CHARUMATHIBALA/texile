Use the **same login form** as regular users (Login button in navbar).

After logging in as admin, you will be redirected to the Admin Dashboard. You can also access it via:
- The **Admin** button in the navbar
- **Admin Dashboard** in the profile dropdown (click your profile icon)

## What Admins Can Do
- Manage products, categories, orders, users
- Upload product images
- Manage discounts & offers
